⚡ Lightning Machine

A real-time 3D + time lightning simulation. The leader grows through a solved electric field (dielectric breakdown model with Gaussian space-charge noise), each breakdown is weighted by Fowler–Nordheim / WKB quantum tunnelling, return-stroke current follows the Heidler/MTLE model, the channel colour is Planck black-body radiation at its temperature, and thunder is synthesised from the channel's geometry in surround sound.

Physics core: Rust → WebAssembly (SIMD). Rendering: WebGL2 HDR. Best with headphones or a 5.1 system.
